Heatwave warning extended across southern Europe
BBC World · Sat 29 Aug 07:40
A heatwave warning has been extended across much of southern Europe as forecasters expect temperatures to stay above 40C into the weekend.
Authorities in Spain, Italy and Greece have urged people to stay indoors during the hottest hours and to check on elderly neighbours.
Wildfire risk remains very high in several regions. Rail operators have reduced speeds on some lines because of track expansion.
The warning is expected to be reviewed on Sunday, when a cooler air mass is forecast to move in from the Atlantic.
